5. The Batch Zero attestation deadline is **reported inconsistently as 15 July 2026 or 24 July 2026**. We never quote either. If a prospect raises it, we say the public reporting conflicts and we will confirm from the filing.

## 2. THE FIVE FREE SAMPLE PACKAGES

We give away five finished gap assessments. Five, not six. The cap is the experiment.

### 2.1 Selection criteria (all must hold)

| Rule | Reason |
|---|---|
| All five score ≥ 70 (Tier A) | We are testing the segment we intend to sell to, not an easy segment |
| 2 from the **373 excluded** projects | Their problem is building an evidence file good enough to get back in |

| No two share a TDSP | Proof travels through the connecting utility; we need to see format variance across utilities |
| Each has a named procurement, electrical engineering or energy contact (field 32), not counsel only | We are testing whether an engineer finds it useful |

### 2.2 Scope and time

- **Scope of the free item:** a document-by-document gap assessment against the supporting-document list in ERCOT's 20 August 2026 presentation — what is required, what they have, what is missing, who outside their company has to sign it, and the order of operations — plus **one** completed evidence exhibit, fully cited.
- **Time:** 10–12 hours of one founder's time, inside five business days. **Hard cap 12 hours.** At 12 hours you ship what exists with the remaining gaps marked open. Total across all five: 60 hours maximum.
- Not included free: assembly of the full file, TDSP-format conformance, or any contact with the TDSP on their behalf.

### 2.3 What we are actually testing

Five hypotheses, each with the observation that kills it. Record the result in the scoreboard notes.

| # | Hypothesis | Confirmed by | Falsified by |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | The gap is real: conditional and excluded projects cannot produce ERCOT's listed documents on demand | ≥3 of 5 assessments find ≥2 document types absent | ≥3 of 5 reply that they already hold the full set — in which case the $45,000 package has no buyer and §5 Gate 2 will fail honestly |
| H2 | The TDSP is the live submission path and imposes a format | ≥1 recipient sends us their TDSP's template or names a format requirement | Nobody can tell us how submission works, which means the routing claim is unconfirmed and must be marked unverified in all copy |
| H3 | The package travels internally. The forward is the signal, not the reply | ≥2 of 5 threads gain a new internal recipient within 10 business days | Replies stay with one person — we are talking to someone with no internal pull |
| H4 | $45,000 is inside the tolerance of the person who reads it | ≥2 of 5 ask for a quote, a scope or the vendor-onboarding process within 10 business days | Interest with no procurement motion means we are a free service |
| H5 | Multi-program is common enough to support a desk | ≥2 of 5 confirm 2 or more active programs | Everyone has one program — then the retainer should not be sold at all and we say so (§4) |

**Cost test, run alongside:** log actual hours per free package. If the free subset consistently exceeds 12 hours, the $45,000 fixed price is mispriced. Record it; **do not change the price inside 90 days** — a changed price corrupts Gate 2. Reprice on Day 91.

## 4. QUALIFICATION SCRIPT — ONE CALL, 20 MINUTES

Open with the boundary, in these words: *"I build and assemble the document set, and I keep the evidence log. I don't sell equipment, I don't take title, and I won't give you a delivery date. If what you need is a lead-time index, VAWN and Terrapin already publish those and I'll point you at them."*

**A. Is there a real December problem? (6 minutes)**

1. Where does [project] stand in Batch Zero — conditional classification, excluded, or not in it?
2. Has [TDSP] come back to you with a document request? What exactly did they ask for?
3. Who inside your company holds the response obligation if ERCOT or [TDSP] asks again next week?
4. Is there an audit report due for this project, and has anyone started it? *(Community Impact Audit threshold is 25 MW; reports reported due 10 Dec 2026 — confirm against the docket, do not assert.)*
5. If this project were disqualified, what happens to the schedule? *(If the answer is "nothing much", there is no December problem.)*

**B. Is the long-lead exposure real? (4 minutes)**

6. What is your longest-lead item, and what weeks did the OEM actually quote in writing?
7. Is a PO placed? Is a slot reserved, and was a deposit paid? *(10–30% of equipment cost, typically non-refundable.)*
8. Do you hold a delivery schedule document, or an email thread?
9. Is there anything you ordered that you no longer need, or need and cannot get?

**C. Is there budget authority? (5 minutes)**

10. Who signs a $45,000 purchase order for outside work on this project?
11. What is the threshold above which it needs someone else?
12. Is there a vendor onboarding process, and how long does it take?
13. Is there a cost code this lands on, or does it need a budget created?

*If 10 and 13 cannot both be answered on the call, this is not a qualified deal. Ask for the name of the person who can answer and end there.*

**D. Is there more than one program? (3 minutes)**

14. How many ERCOT projects do you have in the queue?
15. How many are in verification or will be in the next six months?
16. Do you have in-house procurement headcount dedicated to equipment?

**E. The close, or the honest no (2 minutes)**

- **2 or more active programs + authority + a real December problem** → send (c) and (d). GO.
- **1 program** → say this, out loud: *"You have one program and one document set. Hire someone — loaded, that is about $180,000 to $220,000 a year, and you will get more out of a full-time person than out of me at $18,000 a month. If you want the one-off evidence package for $45,000 I will do that, and then you should hire."* Then send (c) only, and never send (d). **Single-program prospects are told to hire. We do not sell them the retainer.**
- **No authority, or no pending verification** → PARK. Log in field 50 and move on.

**Not to be said on any call:** either attestation date; any delivery-date prediction; any claim that a novation or order-position transfer has ever been completed (zero instances have been observed); any figure not in Appendix A.

| # | Trap | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Do not build the lead-time data product.** | VAWN and Terrapin Consulting already ship lead-time indices. Our product is the evidence file, not the index. When a prospect asks for lead-time data, cite theirs and move the conversation back to documents |
| 2 | **Do not take title to equipment.** | That market is occupied and capitalised: Anza Exchange (founded 2021, ~86 employees, $0 listing fee at launch, backed by Angeleno Group and Energy Capital Partners), Surplus Record (4,469 transformer listings, >5,000 inquiries in a trailing year), Transformer Exchange Inc, CTG Power Systems, Maddox, J&J Transformers.
