2.1	DEFINITIONS
Large Load
One or more Facilities at a single site with an aggregate peak Demand greater than or equal to 75 MW behind one or more common Points of Interconnection (POIs) or Service Delivery Points.

ERCOT Market Impact Statement | ERCOT Staff has reviewed NPRR1308 and believes the market impact for NPRR1308, along with NOGRR282, provides necessary requirements to reduce the reliability risk posed by LCLs unexpectedly tripping or transferring to backup generation when frequency and voltage excursions within a specified range occur. Without these requirements, events involving LCL loss would increase in magnitude, potentially leading to system frequency and voltage instability.
