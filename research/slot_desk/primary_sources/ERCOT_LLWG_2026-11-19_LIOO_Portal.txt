Load Integration & Ongoing Operations (LIOO) Overview
LIOO is ERCOT's centralized application and data repository for Large Loads
A single location to request, collect, and maintain Large Load information
Supports the Large Load lifecycle from interconnection through ongoing operations
Improved visibility into interconnection status 
ERCOT is working to develop process sequence and hand-offs within the overall process1

===== SLIDE 4 =====
LIOO – Pilot Version Access 

Read-only access for TSPs and ILLEs to view project information and dashboards.

TSPs will access LIOO through their existing USA, consistent with RIOO.

ILLEs will register using their email address associated with the project.

ERCOT Large Load team will enter new projects and project updates during the pilot period, including ILLE contact information/email addresses.
